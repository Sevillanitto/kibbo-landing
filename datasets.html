Consumer Data Library

Every dataset Kibbo has used in its own investigations — where it came from, what it contains, and which piece of reporting it powered.

This isn't a general directory of external datasets. It only lists sources Kibbo has actually used and verified firsthand while building an investigation — one entry per dataset, added the same day the investigation that uses it goes live.

Kibbo Streaming Pricing Dataset, 2011–2026

Source: Kibbo original dataset, built from contemporaneous reporting on each price change (publication listed per row)

97 verified price-change events — launches, increases, the one decrease, and tier restructures — across 10 major US streaming platforms, from each platform's launch through September 2026, with the before and after price and percentage change for every event.

AI-Related Fraud Dataset — US, 2025

Source: FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3), 2025 Annual Report

Reported losses and complaint counts for AI-related fraud — the first year IC3 tracked this as its own category — plus breakdowns by scam type, including investment fraud, business email compromise, romance scams, and distress scams.

Used in:The AI Scam Playbook: How Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Fraud in 2026 → Original source:IC3 2025 Annual Report (PDF) →

US Airline Reliability Dataset — 2026 H1

Source: US Department of Transportation, Air Travel Consumer Report (August 2026 edition)

On-time performance, cancellation rates, mishandled baggage, and involuntary denied boarding data for the 9 major US airlines, January–June 2026.
