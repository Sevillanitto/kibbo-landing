The price on the windscreen is only the start. Add in depreciation, financing interest, insurance, fuel, maintenance and yearly fees to see what owning this car will really cost you — in total, and per month.

By default we use a typical new car, industry-average curve — your specific model may hold value better or worse. From year 6 we assume −5% a year, which is our own estimate rather than part of that data. Enter a single yearly percentage here if you have a better figure for your model.
