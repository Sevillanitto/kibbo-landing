Free Resources

The tools that already exist to protect you

No sign-up. No payment. Just useful information.

Some of the most useful consumer tools cost nothing — you just have to know they exist. The official complaint portal for your mobile carrier, the database of known scam numbers, the regulator that actually has the power to fine a company that wronged you. This information is public, but it’s scattered and buried.

Kibbo’s directory collects it in one place. No sign-up, no payment, no catch — just curated links and references to the free resources that already exist to protect you, organized so you can find the right one fast.
