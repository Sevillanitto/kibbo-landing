Scam Recovery Hub

Been scammed? Start with what happened

Everything Kibbo has for getting your money back and reporting what happened — grouped by scam type, not by resource type, so you can go straight to the ones that fit your situation.

When you've been scammed, the hard part isn't a lack of help — it's that the official reporting portal, the tool that checks the damage, and the letter template you need are all in different places. This hub puts them together by scam type: the resource that has real enforcement power, the tool that does the analysis, and the ready-made document, side by side.

Identity Theft

Someone opened credit, filed taxes, or took out a loan in your name.

Resources

IdentityTheft.gov →The FTC's identity-theft recovery portal — generates a personal recovery plan and affidavit. FTC Report FraudOfficial US portal to report fraud and feed the Consumer Sentinel database. IDCARE (Australia) →Australia's government-supported national identity & cyber support service — builds a response plan for exposed ID documents (licence, passport, Medicare).

Tools

AI Phishing DetectorPaste a suspicious message to check if it's the phishing attempt behind the theft.

Templates

Identity Theft Recovery Action PlanA step-by-step recovery checklist and contact directory for US and UK victims.

Bank Fraud & Chargebacks

Unauthorised charges, a refused refund, or a payment you need reversed.

Resources

CFPB Complaint Portal (US)Put a US bank on a 15-day clock to respond formally. Financial Ombudsman Service (UK)Free, binding UK arbitrator that can overrule your bank. FCA Register (UK)Check a firm is authorised before you trust it with money. Scamwatch — Report a Scam (Australia) →The ACCC / National Anti-Scam Centre reporting portal — feeds national scam intelligence used to disrupt scam infrastructure. ACSC ReportCyber (Australia) →The Australian Cyber Security Centre's official cybercrime report portal — get a formal reference number for police, banks and insurers.

Tools

Legal Contract AuditorScan the T&Cs behind the charge for unfair clauses and dark patterns.

Templates

Chargeback Dispute Evidentiary PackThe dispute letter, evidence checklist and phone script that win a bank chargeback.

Online Shopping Scams

A fake store, an order that never arrived, or an overseas seller who vanished.

Resources

Is This Website a Scam? (ScamDoc guide)How to check a store before you buy — beyond a single trust score. eConsumer.govReport a cross-border online shopping scam to 35+ agencies. Better Business Bureau (US)Check a US company's complaint history before buying.

Templates

Online Shopping Safety ChecklistTwenty fast checks before you hand your card to a store you've never used.

Phishing & Impersonation Scams

A fake text, email or call pretending to be your bank, a courier or a government body.

Resources

Action Fraud (UK)The UK's national reporting centre — start the paper trail your bank needs. FTC Report Fraud (US)Report impersonation and phishing scams to the US federal government.

Tools

AI Phishing DetectorPaste the message for an instant verdict — phishing, suspicious or legitimate.

Templates

Phishing & Scam Incident Report TemplateDocument the scam for your bank, the police or fraud agencies.

Investment & Financial Firm Scams

A "broker", trading platform or adviser that may be a clone or unauthorised firm.

Resources

How to Check the FCA Register (guide)Spot clone firms and unauthorised brokers before you invest. FCA Register (UK)The official record of every firm the FCA authorises or has warned about.
