Travel

Flight delayed or cancelled: how to claim

Know your rights, the deadlines that apply, and the free tools to act on it.

Last verified: 13 October 2026

US UK Europe

Can you claim?

Where did the flight depart from, or which airline operated it?

US: No federal compensation right. You can claim a refund (see rights).
UK: UK departure (any airline) or UK/EU airline arriving in the UK: UK261 applies.
Europe: EU departure (any airline) or EU airline arriving in the EU: EU261 applies.

How late did you arrive, or was the flight cancelled?

3+ hours late on arrival, or cancelled, opens compensation under UK261/EU261.

Was it caused by 'extraordinary circumstances' (e.g. weather, air traffic control)?

If yes, no compensation, but care and refund rights remain. Most technical faults and airline-staff strikes do not count as extraordinary (EU page).

Your rights

DOT automatic refund rule

Refund
Full refund (incl. taxes/fees) if the airline cancels or significantly changes the flight and you decline the alternative. Significant change: >3h domestic, >6h international.
Baggage
Checked-bag fee refund if bag not delivered within 12h (domestic) / 15-30h (international).
Timeline
Card payments: within 7 business days. Other methods: within 20 calendar days.
No compensation note
DOT does not require airlines to pay cash compensation for domestic flight delays or cancellations, unlike the UK/EU rules — this rule covers refunds only. Some airlines voluntarily commit to compensation or amenities (meals, hotels, rebooking) for disruptions within their control; check the DOT's Airline Customer Service Dashboard for what a specific airline has committed to. DOT states it will hold airlines accountable to those commitments.

UK261

Compensation
Under 1500km
£220
1500 3500km
£350
Over 3500km
£260 (3-4h late) / £520 (4h+ late)
Threshold
Arrive 3+ hours late
Escalation
Complain to airline; if unresolved after final response or 8 weeks, go to the airline's ADR provider, or CAA PACT if the airline has none.
Time limit
The CAA does not publish a UK261-specific claim deadline. The general civil claim time limit applies instead: 6 years from the date of the flight in England and Wales (Limitation Act 1980, the standard limit for breach-of-contract claims), and 5 years in Scotland (Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act 1973, the standard prescriptive period for contractual obligations).

EU261

Compensation
Up to 1500km
€250
1500 3500km
€400 (and >1500km intra-EU)
Over 3500km
€600
Threshold
Arrive 3+ hours late (4h for the €600 tier on some non-EU flights); cancellation notified under 14 days before departure
Care
Meals, 2 calls/messages, hotel + transport if overnight. Refund option at 5h+ departure delay.
Escalation
Airline first; no reply in 2 months -> national enforcement body; ADR; European Small Claims.
Time limit
Set by each country's national limitation rules (no single number)

Evidence to gather

  • Booking confirmation
  • Boarding pass
  • Screenshot of actual vs scheduled arrival time
  • Airline messages explaining the cause
  • Receipts for meals/hotel/transport

Steps

  1. Keep evidence and receipts
  2. Written claim to the airline
  3. Wait 8 weeks (UK) / 2 months (EU)
  4. Escalate to ADR / CAA PACT / national body
  5. Small claims court
