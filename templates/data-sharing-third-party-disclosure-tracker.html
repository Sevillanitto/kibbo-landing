Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure Tracker

Every "who we share your data with" disclosure a company gives you, logged in one place — with the ones that never actually state a purpose or legal basis flagged automatically.

The problem

"We may share your information with our partners" tells you nothing

A privacy policy or a response to a rights request will often name third parties a company shares your data with, but the disclosures usually arrive piecemeal — one line in a policy update, a vague answer to an access request, a partner mentioned once and never again. Without a running log, there's no way to see the full picture of who actually has your data, or to notice when a disclosure doesn't even bother to state why.

This tracker logs every disclosure as you learn about it — the recipient, the stated purpose, the legal basis the company claims to rely on, and where you found out about it — and calculates a summary that flags any row missing a real purpose or basis, so the gaps in a company's own disclosure are visible at a glance.

Features
  • Disclosure Log — recipient, purpose, legal basis claimed, and source of the disclosure
  • Auto-calculated summary flagging disclosures with no stated purpose or basis
  • Date-tracking for when each disclosure was made or discovered
  • Sortable by recipient, purpose, or legal basis to spot patterns
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel tracker and open the Disclosure Log tab.
  2. Log every third party a company names as a data recipient, along with where you found it (policy, request response, notice).
  3. Record the stated purpose and legal basis for each disclosure, if any is given.
  4. The summary tab automatically flags rows with no real purpose or basis stated.
  5. Use the flagged rows as the basis for a follow-up question or a formal complaint.
Who it's for

Anyone trying to build a complete picture of who has their data and why

Whether you're comparing a privacy policy against what a company actually discloses, or building a case that a disclosure lacks a real legal basis, this keeps the evidence organized.

What's included
  • Editable Excel workbook
  • Disclosure log with recipient, purpose, and legal basis fields
  • Auto-calculated summary flagging incomplete disclosures
  • Works on any device
