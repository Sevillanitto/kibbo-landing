Supplement Label & Claims Verification Worksheet

"Clinically proven" and "supports joint health" sound similar on a label. One is a wellness claim, the other is regulated territory. Check exactly what a product claims — and what it's actually allowed to.

The problem

A confident label isn't the same as a compliant one

Marketing copy and the actual ingredient panel don't always agree — a dose can be lower than what's implied on the front of the pack, and a claim can quietly cross from "supports" into disease-treatment territory. Spotting the gap takes going through the label and the marketing side by side, not just a skim.

This worksheet walks the label and the claims through the same checks a regulator or a savvy buyer would use, before you buy or before you build a complaint.

Features
  • Ingredient/Dosage Cross-Check — compares the front-of-pack claim against the actual ingredient panel and serving size
  • Health-Claims Risk Checklist — sorts each claim into wellness/structure-function language versus disease-treatment language
  • Certification & Testing Verification Table — records which third-party marks are claimed, and whether they check out
How it works

Step by step

  1. Copy the front-of-pack claims and the full ingredient panel into the worksheet.
  2. Cross-check the actual dose per serving against what the marketing implies.
  3. Sort every health claim using the risk checklist — wellness language or disease-treatment language.
  4. Verify any certification mark claimed on the label against the certifying body's own records.
Who it's for

Anyone checking a product before buying, or building a false-advertising complaint

Works for a quick pre-purchase check or as the documented evidence behind a complaint to a retailer, manufacturer, or advertising regulator.
