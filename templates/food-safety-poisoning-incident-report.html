Document a suspected food poisoning incident properly, before the details go fuzzy.

The problem

Symptoms start hours later, after the details have blurred

Food poisoning rarely announces itself immediately — symptoms often start 6 to 48 hours after the meal, by which point exactly what you ate, when, and who else shared the meal has started to blur.

This report gives you a structured place to log the meal, the timeline, and the symptoms while they're still fresh, so you have something accurate to hand a health department, the restaurant, or a delivery platform.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Log what you ate, where, and exactly when, while it's still fresh in memory.
  2. Track when symptoms started and how they progressed, hour by hour.
  3. Note anyone else who shared the meal and whether they got sick too.
  4. Work through the reporting checklist — your local health department and the restaurant or platform both matter.
Who it's for

Anyone who suspects food poisoning from a meal or delivery order

Restaurant meal, delivery order, or packaged food — if you think it made you sick, an accurate record is what turns a suspicion into a report someone can act on.

What's included
  • Meal and timeline log (what, where, when)
  • Symptom tracker with onset and progression
  • Other-diners-affected log
  • Reporting checklist — health authority and restaurant/platform — delivered as PDF
