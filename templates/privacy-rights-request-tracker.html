The problem

Five companies, five deadlines, one spreadsheet you don't have

Cleaning up your privacy footprint rarely means sending one request and being done — it usually means an access request here, a deletion there, a correction somewhere else, each with its own deadline and its own clock that started ticking the moment the company received it. Track it in your head or your inbox and it's easy to lose track of which one is actually overdue.

This tracker holds every request in one sheet — the right being exercised, the company, the date sent — and calculates the days remaining against each deadline automatically, with a summary that flags anything already overdue so nothing quietly falls through.

Features
  • Multi-Request Log — access, deletion, correction, objection, restriction, tracked side by side
  • Auto-calculated days remaining per deadline
  • Auto-calculated summary flagging overdue requests
  • Status column for acknowledged, in progress, resolved, or escalated
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel tracker and log each request — the company, the right exercised, and the date sent.
  2. Enter the applicable deadline for each request based on the jurisdiction and right involved.
  3. The days-remaining column updates automatically as time passes.
  4. Check the summary tab regularly to see which requests are approaching or past their deadline.
  5. Update the status column as each request moves from acknowledged to resolved or escalated.
Who it's for

Anyone managing more than one open privacy rights request

Whether you're doing a full privacy cleanup across several companies or just keeping two or three requests straight, this keeps every deadline visible in one place.

What's included
  • Editable Excel workbook
  • Multi-request log with auto-calculated deadline tracking
  • Auto-calculated overdue-request summary
  • Works on any device
