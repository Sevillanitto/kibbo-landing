The problem

A semester abroad has a lot of moving parts, in different systems, in a different country

Visa paperwork, insurance policy details, host university contacts, accommodation terms, and payment records end up scattered across emails, apps, and a home institution's portal — and if something goes wrong once you're actually there, tracking down the right document from a different time zone is its own problem. This kit puts everything in one place before you leave, so it's there when you need it.

How it works
