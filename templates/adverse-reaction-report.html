Adverse Reaction Report

A supplement, medicine, or health product caused a reaction you didn't expect. Get the facts down properly before you report it — to the seller, the manufacturer, or a safety authority.

The problem

A vague description won't get you taken seriously

"It made me feel sick" doesn't give a manufacturer, retailer, or safety authority anything to act on. What actually matters is the exact product and batch, precisely when symptoms started relative to when you took it, what medical care you needed, if any, and what evidence you kept — recorded once, factually, before memory of the details fades.

This report keeps all of that in one place, in the format a seller, manufacturer, or national safety authority actually needs to take a report seriously.

Features
  • Product & Batch Details — exact product name, brand, batch/lot number, and where and when it was purchased
  • Symptom Timeline — when the product was taken, when symptoms started, and how they progressed
  • Medical Treatment Record — any care received, from self-treatment to emergency treatment
  • Evidence Checklist — packaging, receipts, photos, and other proof worth keeping before you report
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the product and batch details while the packaging is still in front of you.
  2. Log the symptom timeline as accurately as you can — exact times matter more than they seem to at first.
  3. Note any medical treatment received, even if it was minor or self-administered.
  4. Work through the evidence checklist, then attach the completed report to your complaint to the seller, manufacturer, or the relevant safety authority.
Who it's for

Anyone who's had an unexpected reaction to a supplement, medicine, or health product

Covers the US, UK, EU, and Australia — the report itself isn't country-specific, so it works as the factual record behind a report to any national health or medicines safety authority.
