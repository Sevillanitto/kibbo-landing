The problem

Fee disputes get lost without the numbers in writing

Tuition billing errors — an unexpected surcharge, a fee that doesn't match what was quoted at enrollment, a charge that should have been waived — are hard to dispute convincingly without laying the exact billed amount against the exact expected amount, side by side, with the difference already calculated. This toolkit builds that comparison for you, plus the payment history and communication record an institution's billing office or a regulator actually wants to see.

Features
  • Fee Breakdown with automatic billed-vs-expected calculation
  • Contract Summary to record what you were actually promised
  • Payment Tracker and Refund Eligibility worksheet
  • Complaint Timeline and Communication Log
  • Evidence Checklist and Escalation Tracker
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel workbook.
  2. Enter what you were billed and what you were told you'd owe in the Fee Breakdown — the difference calculates automatically.
  3. Log your Payment Tracker history and check the Refund Eligibility worksheet against your situation.
  4. Build your Complaint Timeline and Communication Log as you raise the dispute.
  5. Use the Evidence Checklist and Escalation Tracker if the institution's billing office doesn't resolve it directly.
Who it's for

Any student disputing a tuition or fee charge

Unexpected surcharges, fees that don't match what was quoted at enrollment, or charges you believe should have been waived or refunded.

  • Evidence Checklist, Escalation Tracker
