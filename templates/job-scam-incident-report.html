The problem

Job scams move fast, and so do the scammers

Postings get deleted, accounts get suspended, and "recruiters" disappear the moment you ask the wrong question. This template gives you one place to capture everything before it's gone: recruiter and company details, every payment request (even ones you didn't pay), and a checklist of exactly what evidence to screenshot and save. It's built to feed directly into a report to the FTC or the FBI's IC3, or to the job platform where the posting appeared.

Features
  • Structured for exactly what the FTC and IC3 ask for
  • Payment request log — valuable evidence even if you never paid
  • Evidence checklist so nothing gets lost before you report it
  • Instant download, editable PDF/Word
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the template (PDF or Word version included).
  2. Fill in the recruiter and company details as soon as something feels off.
  3. Log every payment request, even ones you refused.
  4. Work through the evidence checklist before the posting or account disappears.
  5. Submit your completed report to the FTC, IC3, and the platform involved.
Who it's for

Anyone contacted with a job offer that feels off

Applicants targeted by fake recruiters, check-cashing scams, or "pay for training/equipment" postings that ask for money or personal information before a real job even starts.

What's included
  • Print-ready PDF report
  • Editable Word version
  • Recruiter and company detail fields
  • Payment request log
  • Evidence checklist
  • Where-to-report guidance (FTC, IC3, job platform)
  • Works on any device
