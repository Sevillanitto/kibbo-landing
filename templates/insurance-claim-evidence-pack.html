Insurance Claim Evidence Pack

An insurance claim moves faster, and gets paid more fairly, when you have the complete file organized in one place from day one — not scrambled together after the insurer pushes back.

The problem

An insurance claim without an organized file favors the insurer, not you

An insurance claim is a paperwork process before it's anything else. Policy details, what actually happened, the evidence that supports it, every call and email with the adjuster, what you've spent, and where the claim currently stands — if any of that is scattered or missing, the insurer's version of events is the one that sticks.

This pack builds the complete file from the start: your policy details, a clear incident description, a damage and evidence log, a full communication and timeline log, an expenses summary, and a claim status tracker — everything in one place, ready the moment you need to push back or escalate.

Features
  • Policy Details — the coverage, limits, and policy number you're claiming against, in one place
  • Incident Description — what happened, when, and how, recorded clearly and dated
  • Damage/Evidence Log — every piece of evidence, cross-referenced to the incident
  • Communication & Timeline Log — every call, email, and letter with the insurer, in order
  • Expenses Summary — what the incident has cost you so far
  • Claim Status Tracker — where the claim actually stands right now, and what's next
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record your policy details and confirm exactly what coverage applies.
  2. Describe the incident clearly, with the date, location, and what happened.
  3. Log every piece of damage or evidence as you gather it, cross-referenced to the incident.
  4. Track every communication with the insurer — date, method, who, and what was said.
  5. Keep a running expenses summary and update the claim status tracker as the claim progresses.
Who it's for

Anyone with an active insurance claim, from filing through to settlement

Home, auto, travel, or any other insurance claim where the outcome depends on being able to show, clearly and in order, what happened and what the insurer was told.
