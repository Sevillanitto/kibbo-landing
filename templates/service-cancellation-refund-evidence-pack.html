Service Cancellation & Refund Evidence Pack

A cancellation you can prove you sent, and a refund claim you can actually back up — not just a memory of when you think you clicked cancel.

The problem

"I cancelled" isn't evidence — a dated record is

Providers routinely dispute that a cancellation happened, or claim it was received after a charge had already been triggered. Without your own record of exactly when you cancelled, what method you used, and what you were told, that dispute becomes your word against theirs — and a charge that continued after cancellation, or a refund you're clearly owed, gets harder to prove the longer it drags on.

This pack keeps the whole sequence in one place — the original service details, why you cancelled, every cancellation request you sent, any charges that landed during the cancellation window, your refund request and its status, and every communication with the provider — so the evidence exists before you need it, not reconstructed from memory afterward.

Features
  • Original Service Details — what you signed up for, when, and at what price
  • Reason for Cancellation — recorded clearly and specifically, not vaguely
  • Cancellation Request Log — every attempt, dated, with the method used
  • Payments During the Cancellation Window — charges that landed after you cancelled
  • Refund Request Tracking — amount claimed, date requested, current status
  • Communications Log — every call, email, and chat with the provider
  • Evidence Checklist — what to save before it disappears
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and fill in the original service details and your reason for cancelling.
  2. Log every cancellation request you send, with the date, method, and any confirmation reference.
  3. Record any charge that lands after you cancelled, alongside your refund request and its status.
  4. Keep the Communications Log updated with every contact, so the full sequence is visible at a glance.
  5. Work through the Evidence Checklist before you escalate, so nothing is missing when it matters.
Who it's for

Anyone cancelling a subscription and disputing a charge or refund

Whether the provider disputes the cancellation happened, or a refund is simply taking longer than it should, this keeps the dated record that dispute actually needs.
