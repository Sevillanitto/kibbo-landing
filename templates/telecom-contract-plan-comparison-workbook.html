The problem

Two plans at the same price can cost very different amounts

A monthly price is the number every plan leads with, but it rarely includes the equipment rental, the data-cap overage rate, the roaming charges, or what it actually costs to leave before the contract ends. Comparing plans on price alone means finding those details out after you've already signed.

This workbook lines up every plan you're considering on the full contract picture — not just the headline price — with a built-in red-flags checklist for the terms that matter most.

Features
  • Plan Comparison Table — monthly price, contract length, and data/minutes allowance, side by side
  • Full Cost Breakdown — equipment fees, activation costs, and overage rates for each plan
  • Cancellation Terms Tracker — notice period and early termination fee for each option
  • Red-Flags Checklist — the contract terms worth double-checking before you sign
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the workbook and add each plan you're considering.
  2. Fill in the full cost breakdown for each — not just the monthly price.
  3. Log the cancellation terms and notice period for each option.
  4. Work through the red-flags checklist before you sign anything.
Who it's for

Anyone comparing mobile, internet, or phone plans before signing up

Whether you're choosing between two providers or renewing with your current one, this compares the full contract cost, not just the advertised price.
