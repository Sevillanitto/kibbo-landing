The problem

The most common return dispute is missing exactly one thing

Most return-and-refund disputes don't come down to whether you're telling the truth — they come down to whether you can prove the seller actually received the item back. A seller who claims never to have received a return has little reason to process a refund, and by the time the dispute escalates, the postage receipt is often long gone.

This pack keeps that proof intact from the moment you start the return — the seller's own return instructions, your proof of postage, delivery confirmation showing the seller received it, and a running record of the refund's status.

Features
  • Return Instructions Record — exactly what the seller asked you to do, and by when
  • Proof of Postage — the receipt that shows you actually sent it
  • Delivery Confirmation — proof the seller received the return
  • Refund Tracking — amount expected, date requested, and current status
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and save the seller's return instructions before you send anything back.
  2. Keep your proof of postage the moment you send the return.
  3. Save delivery confirmation once tracking shows the seller received it.
  4. Track the refund amount, request date, and status until it's resolved.
Who it's for

Anyone waiting on a refund for a return already sent

Whether the seller is slow or genuinely claims not to have received it, this keeps the one piece of proof most disputes actually need.

What's included

  • Return instructions and postage/delivery proof record
  • Refund tracker
  • Works on any device
