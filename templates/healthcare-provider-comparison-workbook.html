Healthcare Provider Comparison & Decision Workbook

Choosing a provider often happens under time pressure, comparing a few names with little more than a phone call. Compare providers side by side on price, credentials, and policies before you decide — with a built-in scoring formula so the comparison isn't just a gut feeling.

The problem

A rushed decision rarely compares more than one provider at a time

Price, credentials, and cancellation or refund policies rarely get compared side by side in any structured way — most decisions happen one phone call or one website at a time, under time pressure, with nothing written down to compare afterward. That makes it easy to pick based on whoever you spoke to last, rather than whoever's actually the better fit.

This workbook gives you a single grid to compare multiple providers at once, with a built-in formula to help weigh the factors that matter to you.

Features
  • Multi-Provider Comparison Grid — price, credentials, and policies side by side
  • Auto-Calculating Decision Score — a weighted score across your chosen factors
  • Credentials & Verification Checklist — what to actually check before committing
  • Price & Policy Notes — cancellation, refund, and other terms in one place
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the workbook (PDF or Excel version included).
  2. List each provider you're considering in the Comparison Grid.
  3. Enter price, credentials, and policy details for each one.
  4. Let the built-in formula calculate a decision score across the factors you enter.
  5. Use the score alongside your own judgment to make the final call.
Who it's for

Anyone comparing multiple healthcare providers before committing

Especially useful for elective or higher-cost treatment, where the decision genuinely benefits from being deliberate rather than made on the spot.
