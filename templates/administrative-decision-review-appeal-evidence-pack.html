The problem

An appeal is a different case than the original complaint

Once a government agency has issued a formal written decision, challenging it isn't the same process as making a general complaint anymore — there's usually a specific appeal procedure, a stated deadline, and a requirement to identify the actual grounds you're appealing on, not just that you disagree with the outcome.

This pack is specifically for that stage — after a written decision exists, not the general complaint that may have preceded it.

Features
  • Grounds for Appeal — the specific legal or factual basis for challenging the decision
  • Evidence Log — every document and piece of evidence supporting your appeal, organized and referenced
  • Appeal Steps & Deadlines — the specific procedure and dates that apply to this decision
  • Outcome Tracking — the appeal's status and any further escalation steps available
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the decision date and the specific appeal deadline that applies — this is the most time-sensitive step.
  2. State your grounds for appeal clearly and specifically, not just general disagreement with the outcome.
  3. Log every piece of supporting evidence, referenced against the specific ground it supports.
  4. Track the appeal's progress and outcome, including any further escalation route if the appeal doesn't succeed.
Who it's for

Anyone who has already received a formal written decision they want to challenge

A benefits denial, a permit refusal, a fine, or any other formal government decision — for the appeal stage specifically, once the decision is already in writing.
