The problem

No bank statement shows you the full recurring picture

A bank or card statement lists individual charges in the order they landed — not the full picture of every recurring payment leaving your account each month, what it adds up to over a year, or which of those payments you've actually stopped using. Small, forgettable amounts are easy to overlook one at a time, and a duplicate service (two music subscriptions, an old cloud storage plan you replaced but never cancelled) can sit there indefinitely.

This tracker builds one full inventory of every recurring payment leaving your account, calculates the annual cost per subscription automatically, and tracks actual usage so the forgotten and duplicate services are easy to spot — with a summary that totals your real annual recurring spend and flags the clearest cancellation candidates.

Features
  • Full inventory of every recurring payment leaving your account
  • Auto-calculated annual cost per subscription
  • Usage tracking to spot forgotten or duplicate services
  • Auto-calculated summary — total annual recurring spend and cancellation candidates
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel workbook and open the Inventory tab.
  2. List every recurring payment you can find — bank statement, card statement, App Store/Play Store subscriptions, and email receipts.
  3. Enter the billing amount and frequency for each; the annual cost calculates automatically.
  4. Rate how often you actually use each one, so forgotten and duplicate services stand out.
  5. Review the summary for your total annual recurring spend and the flagged cancellation candidates.
Who it's for

Anyone who suspects their recurring payments add up to more than they realize

Especially useful if you've switched services, shared accounts with someone else, or simply never sat down and listed everything leaving your account each month.
