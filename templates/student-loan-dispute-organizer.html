Student Loan Dispute Organizer

Keep your loan servicer's record honest — payments, interest, and every conversation, in one place.

The problem

Servicer records don't always match what actually happened

A misapplied payment, an interest miscalculation, or a servicer transfer that loses track of your history are common — and hard to dispute if your own records are scattered across statements, emails, and phone calls you didn't write down. This organizer keeps your own parallel record of every payment, every rate, and every conversation, so a discrepancy is provable rather than a "your word against theirs" argument.

Features
  • Loan Summary to keep your account details in one place
  • Payment History tracker independent of the servicer's own statements
  • Interest Tracker to check rate changes and accrual against what you were told
  • Communication Log for every call, email, and letter
  • Complaint History and Escalation Timeline
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel workbook.
  2. Fill in the Loan Summary and start logging every payment in the Payment History tracker.
  3. Use the Interest Tracker to check accrual and rate changes against your loan terms.
  4. Log every servicer conversation in the Communication Log as it happens, not after the fact.
  5. If a discrepancy needs to be disputed, use the Complaint History and Escalation Timeline to track it through to resolution.
Who it's for

Any student loan borrower disputing a servicer error

Misapplied payments, interest miscalculations, servicer transfers that lose account history, or any discrepancy between what you were told and what actually shows on your account.

  • Communication Log
  • Complaint History, Escalation Timeline
