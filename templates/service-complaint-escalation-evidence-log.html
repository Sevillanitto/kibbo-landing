The problem

Every stage resets the story if you let it

A dispute with a subscription or service provider rarely gets solved by the first person you speak to. It moves — front-line support, then a formal complaint, then a manager, and sometimes an ombudsman or regulator — and at each stage, you're asked to explain the whole thing again, often to someone with no record of what the last person already agreed to.

Without your own timeline, promises made at an earlier stage quietly disappear, deadlines slip without consequence, and each escalation starts from zero instead of building on what came before. This log keeps the full sequence — every contact, every promise and deadline, and exactly which stage you're at — in one place, so the record exists no matter how many times you have to explain it.

Features
  • Communications Timeline — every call, chat, and email, dated and in order
  • Promises & Deadlines Tracker — what the provider committed to, and whether they kept it
  • Multi-Stage Escalation Tracker — front-line → formal complaint → manager → ombudsman/regulator
  • Evidence Checklist — what to save at each stage before you move to the next
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the log and record your first contact in the Communications Timeline.
  2. Log every promise or deadline you're given, and check back to see whether it was actually kept.
  3. Move through the Escalation Tracker as the dispute progresses — front-line, formal complaint, manager, ombudsman/regulator.
  4. Work through the Evidence Checklist before each escalation, so nothing is missing when you move up a stage.
  5. Use the completed log as your evidence file for a formal complaint or regulator submission.
Who it's for

Anyone stuck in a dispute that keeps bouncing between departments

If a complaint about any subscription or service provider has already gone past the first phone call, this keeps the record every subsequent stage will ask you to produce.
