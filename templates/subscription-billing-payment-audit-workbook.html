Every charge a single subscription has ever taken from your account, checked against what you actually expected to pay — with the math done for you.

The problem

A billed amount you never checked line by line

Subscription pricing rarely stays exactly where it started. A promo period ends, a price quietly increases, a discount stops applying, or a charge simply doesn't match what the sign-up page said — and unless you're checking every single statement line against what you actually agreed to, a real discrepancy can sit there for months before anyone notices it.

This workbook lays out every payment a single subscription has taken from your account, calculates the gap between what was charged and what you expected automatically, and keeps a separate log of whether you were actually given advance notice each time the price changed — the exact record a billing dispute needs.

Features
  • Payment Log with auto-calculating discrepancy column — amount charged vs. amount expected
  • Price-Change Log — tracking whether you were given advance notice each time
  • Auto-calculated summary of total discrepancies
  • Flagged rows highlight exactly where the numbers don't match
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the Excel workbook and open the Payment Log tab.
  2. Enter each charge from your bank/card statement, along with the amount you actually expected to be billed.
  3. The discrepancy column calculates the gap automatically and flags any row that doesn't match.
  4. Log any price change in the Price-Change Log, noting whether advance notice was actually given.
  5. Use the auto-calculated summary as the basis for a billing dispute or refund request.
Who it's for

Anyone who suspects a subscription is charging more than it should

Whether it's a promo price that never reverted correctly, a silent increase, or a charge that just doesn't add up, this puts real numbers behind the suspicion.
