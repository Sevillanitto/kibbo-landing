The problem

Appeals get rejected on procedure as often as substance

A university appeals panel isn't just weighing whether your grade or decision was wrong — it's checking whether you followed the process: right grounds, right deadline, right evidence attached in the right place. A strong case presented late, or without a documented timeline and mark calculation in writing, gets the same outcome as a weak one. This toolkit doesn't argue your case for you, but it gives you one place to build the paper trail a panel actually expects to see.

Features
  • How to Use guide plus context on common grounds for appeal
  • Timeline of Events to lay out exactly what happened, in order
  • Evidence Tracker so nothing you've gathered gets lost or forgotten
  • Grade Calculation Worksheet with automatic mark-difference calculation
  • Communication Log and a Deadline Tracker for every step of the process
  • Appeal Submission Checklist and Document Checklist before you file
  • Outcome Tracker and Notes pages
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the toolkit (PDF or Excel version included).
  2. Read the How to Use guidance and identify which grounds for appeal apply to your case.
  3. Build your Timeline of Events and log evidence in the Evidence Tracker as you gather it.
  4. If you're disputing a grade, fill in the Grade Calculation Worksheet — the mark difference calculates automatically.
  5. Work through the Document Checklist and Appeal Submission Checklist before you file, and use the Deadline Tracker to stay ahead of every date.
Who it's for

Any student preparing a formal academic appeal

Grade disputes, exclusion or suspension appeals, extenuating circumstances claims, or any decision a university's own appeals process allows you to challenge.
