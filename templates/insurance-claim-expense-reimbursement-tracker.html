Insurance Claim Expense & Reimbursement Tracker

Out-of-pocket costs from an insurance incident add up fast, and pile up in receipts. This tracker keeps a running total of what's been claimed, reimbursed, and still owed.

The problem

Out-of-pocket costs are easy to lose track of, and easy for an insurer to under-reimburse

Temporary accommodation, emergency repairs, replacement essentials, transport — an insurance incident generates out-of-pocket costs that show up over weeks, not all at once. Without a running record, it's genuinely hard to know what you've spent, what you've already claimed, what's actually been reimbursed, and what's still outstanding.

This tracker logs every expense as it happens, with a running total of what's been claimed, what's been reimbursed, and what's still outstanding — so nothing quietly falls through the gap between "submitted" and "paid."

Features
  • Expense Log — every out-of-pocket cost, dated and categorized
  • Claimed vs. Reimbursed — tracks what's been submitted separately from what's actually been paid
  • Outstanding Balance — automatically calculated, so nothing gets forgotten
  • Receipt Reference — a place to note where the supporting receipt is filed
  • Category Totals — see spend by category (accommodation, repairs, transport, and so on)
How it works

Step by step

  1. Log each out-of-pocket expense as it happens, with the date and category.
  2. Note when you submit each expense to the insurer for reimbursement.
  3. Update the tracker when a reimbursement actually arrives.
  4. Let the workbook calculate your outstanding balance automatically.
  5. Use the running totals to follow up on anything still owed.
Who it's for

Anyone incurring out-of-pocket costs during an active insurance claim

Temporary accommodation, emergency repairs, replacement essentials, or any other cost caused by the incident that you're expecting the insurer to reimburse.
