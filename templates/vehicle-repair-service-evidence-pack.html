Vehicle Repair & Service Evidence Pack

A repair that goes wrong is hard to dispute if you never wrote down what was actually agreed. This pack tracks it from the first symptom to the final bill.

The problem

"They said it would fix it" is hard to prove after the fact

A repair shop's diagnosis, what they said they'd fix, and what you actually authorized can all get fuzzy in memory once a repair doesn't solve the problem or a bill is higher than expected. Without a written record, it's your recollection against the shop's.

This pack records the full arc of a repair: the initial problem, the diagnosis given, what you actually authorized, the parts and labor performed, and the final outcome — so there's a clear record if the repair doesn't hold up.

Features
  • Initial Problem Record — the symptom or issue as first reported, dated
  • Diagnosis Log — what the shop said was wrong, in their own words
  • Authorization Record — exactly what work you approved, and at what cost
  • Parts & Labor Detail — what was actually done, matched against what was authorized
  • Outcome Tracking — whether the repair actually resolved the issue
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the initial problem as you experienced it, with the date and any symptoms.
  2. Log the shop's diagnosis in their own words, not a paraphrase from memory later.
  3. Record exactly what work you authorized and the quoted cost, before work begins.
  4. Check the parts and labor actually performed against what was authorized.
  5. Track whether the repair actually resolved the original issue, and note if it didn't.
Who it's for

Anyone having a vehicle repaired, especially for a costly or recurring issue

A single significant repair, or a recurring problem across multiple visits where keeping a consistent record matters.
