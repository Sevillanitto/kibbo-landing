Government Correspondence & Communication Log

The problem

Every issue has its own log — until you have several at once

Each of Kibbo's other government-facing templates already tracks the communications specific to its own use case — a complaint, an application, a benefits claim, an appeal, a billing dispute. That's exactly right for a single issue. It falls apart the moment you're dealing with more than one at the same time, or with the same agency across different matters, and need one place to see everything, in order, regardless of which issue it belongs to.

This log is that master record — complementary to the communications sections in Kibbo's other five government templates, not a duplicate of them.

Features
  • Master Chronological Log — every call, email, letter, and meeting with any government agency, in one running timeline
  • Cross-Issue Tagging — tag each entry by which application, complaint, or matter it belongs to, so you can filter or view everything at once
  • Contact Reference — who you spoke with, their role, and direct contact details where you have them
How it works

Step by step

  1. Log every contact with a government agency as it happens — date, method, who you spoke with, and what was said.
  2. Tag each entry with the specific issue or application it relates to.
  3. Use the master timeline to see everything in chronological order, or filter to just one issue when you need it.
  4. Reference this log alongside whichever issue-specific Kibbo template applies to what you're actually dealing with.
Who it's for

Anyone juggling more than one matter with a government agency, or across agencies

Also useful even with a single issue, if you simply want one long-term communications record independent of which specific pack you're using for that issue.

  • Cross-issue tagging and contact reference table
