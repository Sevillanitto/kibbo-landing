Utility Billing Audit Workbook

An estimated reading can quietly inflate a bill for months before anyone notices. Check the numbers against your actual usage before you pay it.

The problem

A rising bill is easy to notice. What's driving it is not

An estimated reading, a tariff that quietly reverted to a higher default rate, or a standing charge that doesn't match what you agreed can all inflate a bill without anything looking obviously wrong at a glance. Catching it takes actually comparing what's billed against what your meter and your contract say — bill by bill, not just once.

This workbook lines up your bills against your actual readings and agreed tariff, with an auto-calculated discrepancy column that flags exactly where the numbers stop matching.

Features
  • Bill-by-Bill Log — period, reading type (actual or estimated), and amount charged
  • Auto-Calculated Discrepancy Column — the gap between billed and expected, flagged automatically
  • Tariff Reference — your agreed unit rate and standing charge, checked against what's actually billed
  • Year-on-Year Comparison — this period against the same period last year, to separate a real increase from an error
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the workbook and enter your tariff details — unit rate and standing charge.
  2. Log each bill as it arrives, noting whether it's based on an actual or estimated reading.
  3. The discrepancy column flags automatically where a bill doesn't match your tariff or usage.
  4. Use the year-on-year view to check whether a spike is a real change or a billing error.
Who it's for

Anyone who wants to check a utility bill is actually correct

Whether you're catching a reverted tariff, an estimated reading, or a standing charge that doesn't match what you agreed, this flags the discrepancy automatically.

What's included
  • Editable Excel workbook
  • Bill log with auto-calculated discrepancy column
  • Tariff reference and year-on-year comparison
  • Works on any device
