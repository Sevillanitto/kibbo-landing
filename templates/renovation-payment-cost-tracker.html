Renovation Payment & Cost Tracker

A renovation budget rarely blows up in one big moment — it creeps, payment by payment, until the running total is a surprise. Track every payment as it happens instead.

The problem

A budget only means something if you're tracking against it

Deposits, progress payments, material costs, and change orders all pull from the same budget — but they rarely get logged in the same place while the project is actually happening. By the time you add it all up at the end, the running total is often a surprise, and it's too late to have caught the overspend early.

This tracker keeps every payment against the budget in real time, so a problem shows up as it develops, not after the project's finished.

Features
  • Payment Log — every payment made, by date, amount, and payment type (deposit, progress payment, materials, change order)
  • Automatic Running Balance — updates as you add each payment, so you always know what's left in the budget
  • Budget-vs-Actual Summary — compares planned budget to actual spend, broken down by payment type
How it works

Step by step

  1. Enter your total budget, broken down by category if you have one.
  2. Log every payment as it happens — date, amount, type, and who it was paid to.
  3. Watch the running balance update automatically after each entry.
  4. Check the budget-vs-actual summary regularly to catch a category running over before the project ends.
Who it's for

Anyone managing payments during an active renovation or home service project

Keeps the running total visible throughout the job, not just reconstructed after the fact from receipts and bank statements.
