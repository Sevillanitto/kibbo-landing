The problem

"Delivered" and "in your hands" are not the same claim

A tracking status marked delivered doesn't mean the parcel actually reached you — a scan can happen at the wrong address, a driver can mark it delivered before it's dropped, or it can simply go missing between the last scan and your door. When a seller, carrier, or card issuer asks you to prove what happened, a vague "I never got it" is far weaker than a dated record of exactly what the tracking shows and what you've already tried.

This pack keeps the whole claim in one place — what you ordered and when, the full tracking history as it actually reads, every step you've taken to locate the parcel, and every communication with the seller or carrier — so the evidence exists before you need it, not reconstructed from memory afterward.

Features
  • Order Details — what was ordered, from where, and the delivery address on file
  • Carrier Tracking History — the full scan-by-scan record, copied before it disappears
  • What You've Already Tried — checked with neighbors, building management, the carrier's local depot
  • Communications Log — every contact with the seller and the carrier, dated
  • Evidence Checklist — what to have ready before you file a formal claim or dispute
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and record the order details and delivery address.
  2. Copy the full tracking history into the pack before it's removed from the carrier's site.
  3. Log what you've already checked — neighbors, safe places, the local depot.
  4. Keep the communications log updated with every contact with the seller and carrier.
  5. Work through the evidence checklist before filing a formal claim or a card issuer dispute.
Who it's for

Anyone whose parcel shows as delivered but never arrived

Whether you're disputing with the seller, filing a carrier claim, or escalating to your card issuer, this keeps the tracking record and your own timeline in one file.
