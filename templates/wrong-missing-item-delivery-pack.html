Wrong / Missing Item Delivery Pack

You ordered three things. Two arrived. Or one arrived, and it's the wrong size entirely. A clean side-by-side record beats a confused email thread every time.

The problem

"Some of it's missing" is not a claim a seller can act on

An incomplete or incorrect order is one of the easiest disputes to resolve — if you can show exactly what was ordered against exactly what arrived. Without that side-by-side record, the conversation drifts into back-and-forth over what you actually bought, what the confirmation email said, and whether the missing item was ever really part of the order.

This pack sets that comparison up cleanly from the start — a table of ordered vs. received items, tracking for anything you have to return, and a log of the claim from first contact to resolution.

Features
  • Ordered vs. Received Table — a clean, side-by-side comparison of every line item
  • Order Confirmation Reference — what the confirmation email actually promised
  • Return Tracking — for anything incorrect that needs to go back
  • Claim Log — every contact with the seller, dated, through to resolution
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and fill in the ordered vs. received table item by item.
  2. Save the original order confirmation alongside it as a reference.
  3. Log a return shipment if the seller needs the wrong item back.
  4. Track every contact with the seller through to resolution.
Who it's for

Anyone with an order that arrived incomplete or wrong

Whether an item is missing entirely or the wrong item showed up, this turns a confusing order into a clear, comparable record.

  • Works on any device
