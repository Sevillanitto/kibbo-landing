Car Dealer Dispute & Evidence Pack

A dealership dispute is won on paperwork, not frustration. This pack builds the complete file — contract, defect, warranty, repair history, and costs — in one place.

Get it free →
The problem

A dealer dispute without an organized file is just your word against theirs

Whether it's a defect the dealer won't fix, a warranty claim they're disputing, or a sale that didn't go as agreed, a dealership dispute is rarely resolved by simply being right — it's resolved by being able to show, clearly and in order, the contract, the problem, and every attempt to fix it.

This pack builds that complete file: the contract itself, the specific defect, what warranty coverage actually applies, every repair attempt, the costs involved, all communications with the dealer, and the resolution you're requesting.

Features
  • Contract & Warranty Record — the sale contract and the exact warranty coverage that applies
  • Defect Documentation — what's wrong, described specifically and dated
  • Repair Attempt Log — every attempt the dealer has made to fix the issue, and the outcome
  • Costs & Communications — what you've paid, and the full record of contact with the dealer
  • Requested Resolution — what you're actually asking for, stated clearly
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the sale contract details and identify the specific warranty coverage that applies.
  2. Document the defect precisely — what's wrong, when it started, and how it affects the vehicle.
  3. Log every repair attempt the dealer has made, with dates and outcomes.
  4. Track any costs you've incurred and keep a full communications record with the dealer.
  5. State your requested resolution clearly — a repair, a refund, a replacement, or something else.
Who it's for

Anyone in a dispute with a dealership over a purchase, defect, or warranty claim

A car that arrived with an undisclosed problem, a warranty claim the dealer is resisting, or repairs that haven't actually fixed the issue.
