The problem

Verbal conversations are easy to dispute after the fact

Most employment disputes — a disputed warning, a wage disagreement, a harassment complaint that went nowhere, a severance negotiation — come down to what was actually said, and when. Verbal conversations are easy for an employer to dispute or reframe after the fact if there's no contemporaneous record. This template gives you one place to log every meaningful conversation as it happens: who you spoke to, what channel, what was said or agreed, and any next step that was promised — plus a quick-reference contacts tab so you're not digging through old emails when it matters.

Features
  • Chronological log built for real employment disputes
  • Tracks promises made and whether they were kept
  • Key Contacts tab so you're never searching old emails under pressure
  • Instant download, editable PDF/Excel
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the template (PDF or Excel version included).
  2. Log every meaningful conversation with your employer as close to the moment as possible.
  3. Note the channel, who you spoke to, and exactly what was said or promised.
  4. Keep the Key Contacts tab updated with names and details.
Who it's for

Anyone managing a live dispute with an employer or recruiter

Employees documenting a disputed warning, a wage disagreement, a harassment complaint, or a severance negotiation where what was said — and when — matters.

What's included
  • Print-ready PDF log
  • Editable Excel version
  • Fields: date, contact, channel, topic, what was said/agreed, next step promised, witnesses
  • Key Contacts tab
  • Works on any device
