Third-Party Liability Claim Pack

Claiming against someone else's insurer means proving your case to a company with no obligation to help you. This pack builds that proof.

The problem

A third-party insurer has no obligation to make your case for you

When you're claiming against another party's insurance — after an accident, an incident on their property, or damage they caused — you're dealing with a company whose only real obligation is to the person who pays their premiums, not to you. Nobody there is going to build your case for you.

This pack builds the complete file yourself: the incident itself, every party involved, the damages you've suffered, your evidence, the full communications record, and exactly what compensation you're seeking.

Features
  • Incident Record — what happened, when, and where, recorded clearly
  • Parties Involved — everyone connected to the incident, with contact and insurance details
  • Damages Log — everything you've lost or been harmed by, itemized
  • Evidence File — photos, reports, and documents, organized and cross-referenced
  • Communications & Compensation Sought — the full record, and exactly what you're claiming
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the incident in detail — date, location, and exactly what happened.
  2. List every party involved, with their contact and insurance details where known.
  3. Log your damages — property, injury, lost time, or other harm caused by the incident.
  4. Organize your evidence and cross-reference it to the incident and damages.
  5. State the compensation you're seeking clearly, and track every communication that follows.
Who it's for

Anyone claiming against another party's insurance rather than their own

An accident caused by someone else, an incident on another party's property, or damage caused by a third party — where you're pursuing their insurer directly for compensation.
