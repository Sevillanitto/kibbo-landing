The problem

"It's spotty sometimes" doesn't hold up. A pattern does

A single dropped call is easy for a provider to write off as one-off interference. What's harder to dismiss is a dated, located record showing the same gap at the same address, week after week — especially when it doesn't match the coverage the provider advertised for that area.

This pack keeps the affected locations, the signal/speed evidence, and every conversation with the provider in one place, so a scattered set of bad days becomes a documented case for a contract exit or a compensation claim.

Features
  • Affected Locations Log — home, work, commute route, and any other spot where service consistently fails
  • Signal & Speed Test Records — dated readings that show the pattern, not just a single bad moment
  • Communications Log — every report to the provider, with reference numbers and what was promised
  • Evidence Checklist — what to have ready before requesting a penalty-free exit or compensation
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and log the specific locations where coverage or data consistently fails.
  2. Record signal strength or speed test results over several days, not just once.
  3. Log every report to the provider, including reference numbers and what was said.
  4. Work through the evidence checklist before requesting a contract exit or compensation.
Who it's for

Anyone with a persistent mobile coverage or service problem

Whether you're building a case for a penalty-free contract exit or a compensation claim, this turns scattered bad experiences into a dated, located record.
