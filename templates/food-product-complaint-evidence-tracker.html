The problem

A complaint without a batch number usually goes nowhere

Contamination, a foreign object, mislabeled allergens, spoiled product on the shelf — a manufacturer or regulator can't act on "something was wrong with this," they need the batch or lot number, the barcode, and where and when it was bought.

This tracker captures exactly that, plus a photo checklist, so your complaint has the identifying details that turn a one-off report into something a manufacturer or food safety authority can actually investigate.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Log the product, brand, and batch/lot number or barcode.
  2. Describe the issue and work through the photo evidence checklist.
  3. Record where and when it was purchased, with proof of purchase.
  4. Log who you reported it to — the manufacturer, the retailer, or a food safety authority — and any reference number.
Who it's for

Anyone with a defective or contaminated food product to report

Whether it's contamination, a foreign object, or a labeling error that matters for an allergy, this captures the identifying details a report needs to be taken seriously.

What's included
  • Product and batch/lot identification log
  • Issue description and photo checklist
  • Proof-of-purchase log
  • Reporting log — manufacturer, retailer, and regulator — delivered as PDF + Excel
