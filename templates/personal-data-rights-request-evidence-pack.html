The problem

Five different rights, and one messy inbox

An access request, a deletion request, a correction, an objection to processing, a restriction — each is a distinct legal right with its own deadline, and companies routinely conflate them, ask for more identity verification than the law requires, or simply let the clock run out while the request sits unanswered.

This pack keeps a single request organized end to end: which specific right you exercised and why, exactly what identity information you provided (and what the company asked for beyond that), the deadline that applies to your request, every follow-up you sent, and the evidence you'd need if the company doesn't respond properly and you have to escalate.

Features
  • Right Being Exercised — access, deletion, correction, objection, or restriction, recorded specifically
  • Identity Verification Log — what you provided, what was requested, and whether it was proportionate
  • Deadline Tracker — the response window that applies to your request and jurisdiction
  • Response Tracking — acknowledgement received, substantive response, and what it actually said
  • Escalation-Ready Evidence Checklist — what to have in hand before you complain further
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and record which specific right you're exercising and the request date.
  2. Log the identity information you provided and note anything the company asked for beyond that.
  3. Track the applicable deadline and any extension notice the company sends.
  4. Record the acknowledgement and substantive response as they arrive.
  5. Work through the evidence checklist before escalating to a regulator or data protection authority.
Who it's for

Anyone exercising a personal data right and expecting it might get pushback

Whether a company is slow, over-asks for verification, or gives an incomplete answer, this keeps the exact record a follow-up or complaint actually needs.
