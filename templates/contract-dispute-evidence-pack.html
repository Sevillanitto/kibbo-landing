Contract Dispute Evidence Pack

A contract dispute is won or lost on whether you can show, clearly and in order, what the contract said and what actually happened. This pack builds that record from day one.

Get it free →
The problem

"They breached the contract" isn't a case — a documented timeline is

Knowing you're right about a contract dispute and being able to prove it are different things. Without a clear record of the problem clause, the timeline, and every communication since, a dispute becomes your word against theirs.

This pack organizes everything a dispute actually needs: the specific clause at issue, what happened and when, every communication, every supporting document, and the resolution you're asking for.

Features
  • Problem Clause Record — the exact contract language at issue, and how it was breached
  • Timeline — every relevant event in order, from the contract's signing to the dispute
  • Breach Details — specifically what was promised and what actually happened
  • Communications & Documents Log — every call, email, and document tied to the dispute
  • Requested Resolution — what you're actually asking for, stated clearly
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the exact clause at issue and quote the contract language directly.
  2. Build a timeline of every relevant event, in order, from signing to the current dispute.
  3. Log every communication and document connected to the dispute as it happens.
  4. State the specific resolution you're seeking — a fix, a refund, a release, or something else.
Who it's for

Anyone in an active or forming dispute over a contract

A vendor who didn't deliver, a client who won't pay, a landlord who broke the lease terms — any contract dispute that might need to be proven, not just asserted.
