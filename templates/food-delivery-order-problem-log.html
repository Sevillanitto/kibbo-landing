The problem

"Missing items" needs specifics, not a memory

A delivery app's support chat wants specifics fast: what was missing, what arrived instead, and photo proof — and it wants it before you've finished being annoyed about a cold, wrong, or incomplete order.

This log gives you a place to record exactly what was ordered versus what arrived, with a photo checklist, so the report you file matches what actually happened.

How it works
