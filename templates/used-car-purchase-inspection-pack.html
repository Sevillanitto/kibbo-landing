The problem

Most used car regrets trace back to a check that got skipped

A test drive and a quick look under the hood catch the obvious problems, not the expensive ones — a rushed inspection, done under a seller's polite pressure to decide quickly, is exactly how a rebuilt-title car or a hidden transmission issue slips through.

This pack forces a complete, structured evaluation: seller and vehicle details verified, documentation checked, every area of the car inspected in order, defects logged as you find them, and a clear buy/negotiate/walk-away decision at the end — not a gut call made standing in a parking lot.

Features
  • Seller & Vehicle Details — who you're buying from and exactly what you're buying, on record
  • Documentation Check — title, registration, and ownership history verified before you inspect anything
  • Physical Inspection by Area — exterior, interior, engine bay, undercarriage, and test drive, checked in order
  • Defects Found & Photo Evidence — every issue logged with supporting photos as you go
  • Buy / Negotiate / Walk-Away Decision — a clear final call based on what you actually found
How it works

Step by step

  1. Verify the seller's identity and confirm the vehicle's basic details match the listing and documents.
  2. Check the title, registration, and ownership history before you inspect a single panel.
  3. Work through the physical inspection area by area, logging every defect as you find it — not from memory afterward.
  4. Photograph anything questionable at the time you find it, with a clear reference to where it is.
  5. Use your complete findings to make a final buy, negotiate, or walk-away decision.
Who it's for

Anyone about to buy a used car, from a dealer or a private seller

A structured pre-purchase inspection you can actually follow on-site, rather than trying to remember what to check while a seller waits.
