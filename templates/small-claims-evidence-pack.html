Small Claims Evidence Pack

Small claims court doesn't require a lawyer — but it does require you to show up organized. This pack builds the case file you'd otherwise be assembling the night before.

The problem

Small claims court rewards preparation, not just being in the right

Small claims procedure is designed to work without a lawyer, but that doesn't mean it works without preparation — a judge or arbitrator still needs a clear, organized account of the parties, the timeline, and the losses you're claiming.

This pack builds that case file: who's involved, what happened and when, the contract or payment evidence, the communications, an itemized account of your losses, and a checklist to confirm you're actually ready to file.

Features
  • Parties & Timeline — who's involved, and every relevant event in order
  • Contract/Payment Evidence — the documents that actually support your claim
  • Communications Log — every relevant call, email, or letter
  • Itemized Losses — a specific, added-up account of what you're claiming, not a round number
  • Pre-Filing Checklist — confirm you have what you need before you file
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the parties involved and build a timeline of the relevant events.
  2. Gather the contract and payment evidence that supports your claim.
  3. Log the relevant communications and itemize your losses with specific figures.
  4. Work through the pre-filing checklist before you submit your claim.
Who it's for

Anyone preparing to file — or respond to — a small claims case

An unpaid invoice, a security deposit, a breach of a smaller contract — the kind of dispute small claims court exists for, without needing a lawyer.
