Hotel Restaurant/Minibar Charge Dispute Worksheet

Compare your folio against what you actually consumed, and build the evidence to dispute an overcharge.

The problem

Minibar charges are billed on an honor system that isn't always accurate

Minibars are often billed automatically the moment a sensor is triggered or a staff member does a visual check — which means an item you moved, sampled, or never touched can still show up as consumed on your final bill.

This worksheet lines up your folio against what you actually ate or drank, so a disputed minibar or restaurant charge comes with a specific, itemized case instead of a vague objection at checkout.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Itemize your final folio, restaurant and minibar charges included.
  2. Compare each line against what you actually consumed.
  3. Let the calculator total the disputed amount.
  4. Use the evidence checklist and dispute notes to prepare your case with the front desk or your card issuer.
Who it's for

Hotel guests disputing a minibar or restaurant overcharge

If your final bill includes a minibar or restaurant charge that doesn't match what you actually had, this gives you the itemized comparison to dispute it properly.

What's included
  • Folio vs. consumption comparison
  • Disputed-item calculator
  • Evidence checklist (minibar photos, receipts)
  • Dispute letter notes field — delivered as Excel
