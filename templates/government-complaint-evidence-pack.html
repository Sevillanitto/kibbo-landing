Government Complaint & Evidence Pack

A government agency responds far better to a complaint that's organized, dated, and specific than to a frustrated phone call. Build the file that gets taken seriously.

Get it free →
The problem

A scattered complaint gets a scattered response

A government agency handling hundreds of complaints responds fastest to the ones that are easy to act on — a clear issue, a dated timeline, and a specific ask. A complaint spread across three phone calls and a half-remembered email thread is much easier for an agency to lose track of, or delay, than one you can hand over as a single, organized file.

This pack is that file, built before a formal written decision exists — for the general complaint stage, not an appeal of a decision you've already received.

Features
  • Issue Details — exactly what happened, which agency and department, and the specific outcome you're seeking
  • Timeline — every relevant date in order, from when the issue started to today
  • Communications Log — every call, email, and letter with the agency, dated, with what was said
  • Documents Checklist — what to gather and attach before you submit
  • Requested Resolution — a clear, specific statement of what you want the agency to do
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the issue details and the specific outcome you want.
  2. Build the timeline in order, from the first relevant date to now.
  3. Log every contact with the agency as it happens, not from memory afterward.
  4. Work through the documents checklist, then submit the complete file with your complaint.
Who it's for

Anyone filing a general complaint against a government agency

For the complaint stage — before a formal written decision has been issued. If you've already received a written decision you want to challenge, that's a different process; see the Appeal Pack below.

  • Editable Word version
