Medical Expenses & Billing Dispute Tracker

The problem

A billed amount that doesn't match what you were told is easy to miss

Medical bills often arrive itemized, confusing, and sometimes weeks after treatment — separately from whatever your insurer said it would cover. Without a record of what was actually billed against what was expected, a real discrepancy blends in with normal billing noise, and an appeal deadline can pass before you've even noticed something's wrong.

This tracker puts every bill, every insurance response, and every dispute in one place, with a built-in formula that calculates the gap between billed and expected for you.

Features
  • Billing Log with auto-calculating billed-vs-expected difference
  • Insurance Response Tracker — what your insurer said, and when
  • Dispute & Appeal Log — each disputed charge and its status
  • Appeal Deadline Tracker — the date you're working against
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the tracker (PDF or Excel version included).
  2. Log each bill as it arrives, along with the amount you expected to be charged.
  3. Let the Excel formula calculate the billed-vs-expected difference automatically.
  4. Record your insurer's response to each charge in the Insurance Response Tracker.
  5. Log any dispute you raise and track the appeal deadline that applies.
Who it's for

Anyone disputing a medical bill or appealing an insurance decision

Whether it's a single charge that doesn't match what you were told, or several bills from one treatment that need to be tracked together, this keeps the full picture — and the deadlines — in one place.

  • Editable Excel version with auto-calculating formula

  • Dispute & Appeal Log, Appeal Deadline Tracker
