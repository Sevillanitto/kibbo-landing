Insurance Denial & Dispute Evidence Pack

A denied or underpaid claim isn't final — it's an argument, and arguments are won with a specific, organized rebuttal, not a general complaint.

Get it free →
The problem

A denial letter is specific — your response needs to be too

An insurer's denial or underpayment isn't random: it cites specific reasons and specific policy clauses. A vague appeal that just says "this isn't fair" rarely moves anything. What actually works is meeting the insurer's stated reasons directly, clause by clause, with the evidence that contradicts them.

This pack builds that response: the insurer's stated reasons for the denial or underpayment, the exact policy clauses they cited, your counter-evidence addressing each one, and the specific remedy you're requesting.

Features
  • Denial Reasons — the insurer's stated reasons, recorded exactly as given
  • Policy Clauses Cited — the exact wording the insurer relied on, for direct comparison
  • Counter-Evidence — your evidence addressing each reason point by point
  • Remedy Requested — what you're actually asking for, stated clearly
  • Timeline & Communications — the full record supporting your dispute
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record the insurer's stated reasons for the denial or underpayment exactly as given.
  2. Note the exact policy clauses they cited to justify the decision.
  3. Build your counter-evidence addressing each reason directly, not generally.
  4. State the specific remedy you're requesting — full payment, a review, or something else.
  5. Attach the supporting timeline and communications record.
Who it's for

Anyone whose insurance claim was denied, or paid out at less than it should have been

A claim rejected outright, or settled at a lower amount than the actual loss — where the insurer's decision cites specific reasons that deserve a specific, evidenced rebuttal.
