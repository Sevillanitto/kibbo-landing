The problem

Scattered emails and calls don't add up to a record anyone can follow

A contract conflict rarely stays contained to one thread — calls, emails, and letters pile up across multiple people and, sometimes, multiple issues at once. Reconstructing what was said and when, months later, is genuinely hard without a running log.

This is a master chronological record: every call, email, and letter, who it was with, what it covered, and where it fits relative to everything else — across every party involved, not just one thread.

Features
  • Master Chronological Log — every call, email, and letter, in date order
  • Party & Channel Tracking — who each communication was with, and how it happened
  • Subject/Issue Tagging — which specific matter each entry relates to, if you're tracking more than one
  • Cross-Party View — a single record spanning every party involved, not one log per contact
How it works

Step by step

  1. Log every call, email, and letter as it happens, with the date, party, and channel.
  2. Tag each entry with the specific issue or matter it relates to.
  3. Keep the log running for the full length of the conflict, not just the start.
  4. Use it as your reference record if the matter escalates to a formal dispute or claim.
