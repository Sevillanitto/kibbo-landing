The problem

A "final" bill isn't automatically correct

Cancelling a service should be the end of it — but a final bill can still charge for days after the agreed cancellation date, miss an equipment return credit, or apply the wrong rate right up to the last invoice. Without your own record of the cancellation date and what was agreed, you're relying on the provider to have gotten it right.

This pack keeps the cancellation confirmation, the equipment return record, and a line-by-line review of the final bill in one place, so you can spot exactly what doesn't add up.

Features
  • Cancellation Record — the confirmed cancellation date, reference number, and channel used
  • Equipment Return Tracking — what was returned, when, and proof of postage or drop-off
  • Final Bill Review Table — line-by-line comparison of what was billed against what was agreed
  • Dispute Log — every charge that doesn't add up, and your correspondence about it
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and record your cancellation confirmation and reference number.
  2. Log any equipment return, with proof of postage or drop-off.
  3. Work through the final bill line by line against what was actually agreed.
  4. Log any discrepancy in the dispute log and track the provider's response.
Who it's for

Anyone whose final bill doesn't match their cancellation

Whether it's a charge for days after cancellation, a missing equipment credit, or the wrong rate applied, this keeps the proof organized before you dispute it.

What's included
  • Print-ready PDF pack

  • Cancellation and equipment-return record
  • Final bill review table and dispute log
  • Works on any device
