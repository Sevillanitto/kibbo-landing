The problem

A marketplace protects you — but only with a real file

Third-party marketplace protections (buyer guarantees, A-to-z style claims, platform-mediated refunds) generally exist to step in when a seller won't cooperate — but they require you to actually show what happened: what the listing promised, what you tried directly with the seller first, and how long you waited. Escalate too early or without a record, and the platform has nothing to act on.

This pack builds that file from the start — a record of the listing as it was presented, every message with the seller, and a tracker for the platform-level escalation once direct contact hasn't worked.

Features
  • Listing Evidence — screenshots and details of what the listing actually promised
  • Seller Communications Log — every message sent and received, dated
  • Platform Escalation Tracker — when you contacted the seller directly, and when you escalated to the marketplace
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and save the original listing details before the seller can edit them.
  2. Log every message with the seller as it happens.
  3. Contact the seller directly first, and record the date and outcome.
  4. If the seller doesn't resolve it, use the tracker to log your platform-level escalation and its status.
Who it's for

Anyone stuck in a dispute with a third-party marketplace seller

Whether the seller stopped responding or is disputing what was promised, this builds the file a platform actually needs to step in.
