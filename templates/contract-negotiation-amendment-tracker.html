The problem

By round three of a negotiation, nobody remembers what was actually agreed

A contract negotiation rarely resolves in one pass — clauses go back and forth across emails, calls, and redlines, and it becomes easy to lose track of which version of a clause is actually the current one.

This tracker keeps one row per clause: the original wording, what you proposed, what came back, who owns the next move, and the final agreed language once it's settled.

Features
  • Original Clause & Proposed Change — what the contract said, and what you asked to change
  • Counter-Proposal Log — what the other party came back with
  • Responsibility & Status — who owns the next move, and where each clause currently stands
  • Final Agreed Wording — the actual language once a clause is settled, so nothing drifts back
How it works

Step by step

  1. Log the original clause and your proposed change for every point you're negotiating.
  2. Record each counter-proposal as it comes in, against the same clause.
  3. Update who's responsible for the next move and the current status after every round.
  4. Lock in the final agreed wording once a clause is settled, so it's on record.
Who it's for

Anyone negotiating a multi-round contract

A vendor agreement, a lease, a service contract, or any deal where clauses go back and forth more than once before they're final.

What's included
  • Excel workbook
  • Per-clause negotiation history
  • Status and ownership tracking
  • Works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers
