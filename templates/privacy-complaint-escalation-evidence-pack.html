The problem

A dispute that outgrew a single email thread

Some privacy problems resolve with one request and one reply. Others don't — the company misses its own deadline, gives a partial or evasive answer, or simply stops responding, and the dispute stretches across weeks with promises made and broken along the way. By the time you need to escalate to a data protection authority or regulator, reconstructing what actually happened from a scattered inbox is its own project.

This pack keeps the whole dispute in one file — what happened and why it matters, every communication in order, the deadlines the company committed to and whether it met them, the specific remedy you're asking for, and a 3-stage escalation tracker so you always know exactly where the complaint currently stands.

Features
  • Incident Overview — what happened, when, and which right or rule is involved
  • Communications Timeline — every message, call, and reply in chronological order
  • Deadlines Made and Missed — what the company committed to, and whether it delivered
  • Remedy Requested — the specific outcome you're asking for, stated clearly
  • 3-Stage Escalation Tracker — company, then ombudsman/regulator, then data protection authority
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the pack and record the incident overview — what happened and the right or rule at stake.
  2. Log every communication as it happens, in order, so nothing gets lost.
  3. Track every deadline the company sets for itself, and note whether it actually met it.
  4. State the specific remedy you want, so every escalation stays focused on the same ask.
  5. Move through the 3-stage escalation tracker as the dispute progresses, from the company to the regulator to the data protection authority.
Who it's for

Anyone whose privacy dispute has gone past a single request-and-reply

Whether the company is stalling, gave a partial answer, or missed its own deadline, this keeps the full timeline ready for the next stage of escalation.
