The problem

The bill doesn't always match the table

An item you never ordered, a round of drinks charged twice, an automatic gratuity added on top of one you already left — restaurant bill errors are common, and they're easy to wave through when the table's waiting on you.

This worksheet lines up what you actually ordered against what you were charged, so the discrepancy is a number, not a feeling — and you walk into the dispute with your case already built.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Itemize what was ordered against every line on the bill.
  2. Flag suspicious, duplicated, or unordered items as they come up.
  3. Let the calculator total the exact discrepancy.
  4. Use the evidence checklist and dispute notes field to prepare your case.
Who it's for

Diners disputing an unexpected restaurant charge

Whether it's a double charge, an item you never ordered, or a gratuity added twice, this gives you the itemized comparison a manager or card issuer will actually look at.

What's included
  • Item-by-item order vs. charge comparison
  • Automatic discrepancy calculator
  • Evidence checklist (menu photo, receipt)
  • Dispute/chargeback notes field — delivered as Excel
