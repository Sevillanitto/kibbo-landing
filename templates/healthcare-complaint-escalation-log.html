The problem

A complaint that drags on for months is easy to lose track of

A formal complaint about care, a provider, or a facility rarely resolves in one conversation. It moves through stages — an initial complaint, a provider response, possibly escalation to a regulator or ombudsman — and each stage has its own contact, date, and outcome. Without a running record, it's easy to lose track of exactly where things stand, what was said, and what's still owed to you.

This log gives you one place to document the complaint from the first contact through to whatever resolution — or non-resolution — comes at the end.

Features
  • Complaint Timeline — the full sequence of events, dated
  • Contact & Correspondence Log — every message and call, with who and when
  • Escalation Stage Tracker — which stage you're at, and what's next
  • Outcome & Resolution Notes — what was ultimately said or offered
How it works

Step by step

  1. Download the log (PDF or Excel version included).
  2. Log the initial complaint — date, what happened, and who you raised it with.
  3. Record every contact and response as it happens in the Correspondence Log.
  4. Track which stage of escalation you're at using the Escalation Stage Tracker.
  5. Note the final outcome, and keep the full record for reference or further escalation.
Who it's for

Anyone filing a formal complaint about care, a provider, or a facility

Especially useful where escalation beyond the provider — to a regulator, ombudsman, or complaints body — is a real possibility, since a clear timeline is exactly what most escalation processes ask for.

What's included
  • Print-ready PDF version
  • Editable Excel version
  • Complaint Timeline, Correspondence Log
  • Escalation Stage Tracker, Outcome Notes
  • Works on any device
