The problem

"That's not what the listing said" only works if you saved the listing

An online listing can be edited or removed entirely after a sale, and a seller's verbal or messaged claims are easy to walk back once you've paid. If the car turns out not to match what was promised, your case depends entirely on what you actually preserved at the time.

This pack captures the original listing, every seller claim, listing photos, and message history — all dated and organized — so you have a clear record of exactly what was represented, not just what you remember.

Features
  • Original Listing Capture — the listing text and photos as they appeared, saved before they can change
  • Seller Claims Log — every specific claim made, in writing or verbally, with the date
  • Message & Communication Archive — the full exchange with the seller, not selective excerpts
  • Discrepancy Record — exactly where what was delivered differs from what was promised
How it works

Step by step

  1. Save the original listing text and every photo the moment you find a car you're interested in.
  2. Log every specific claim the seller makes, whether in writing, by message, or verbally.
  3. Keep the full message and communication history, not just the parts that seem important.
  4. If the car doesn't match, record the specific discrepancy against the specific claim it contradicts.
Who it's for

Anyone buying a used car from an online listing or a seller they don't know well

Private sales and dealer listings alike — anywhere the gap between what's advertised and what's delivered could become a real dispute.
