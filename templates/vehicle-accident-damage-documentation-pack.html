The problem

The details that matter most fade fastest

Immediately after an accident, the details are sharp: exactly where things were, who said what, what the damage actually looked like. Days later, filing an insurance claim, those same details have blurred — and insurers reward specific, dated documentation over a general account.

This pack covers everything worth capturing: the vehicles and parties involved, scene evidence, witnesses, insurance details, and repair costs — organized so nothing important gets lost in the days that follow.

Features
  • Vehicles & Parties Involved — full details on everyone and every vehicle involved
  • Scene Evidence — photos, location, and conditions at the time of the accident
  • Witness Record — contact details and what each witness actually saw
  • Insurance Details — policy information for every party involved
  • Repair Cost Tracking — estimates and actual costs as they come in
How it works

Step by step

  1. Record every vehicle and party involved, with contact and insurance details, as soon as possible.
  2. Photograph the scene, the damage, and the surrounding conditions before anything is moved or cleaned up.
  3. Get contact details from any witnesses, and note what they specifically saw.
  4. Log insurance details for every party, and keep a running record of repair estimates and costs.
Who it's for

Anyone involved in a vehicle accident, however minor it seems at the time

What looks like minor damage can still involve an insurance claim later — documenting it properly from the start makes that process far easier.
