Restaurant Complaint Communication Log

A chronological record of every conversation with a restaurant about your complaint — so nothing "agreed" gets forgotten.

Get it free →
The problem

A verbal promise is easy to make and easy to forget

A manager tells you on the phone that a refund is "being processed." Three weeks later, nobody there remembers the call, let alone the promise.

This log keeps a dated, chronological record of every contact — who you spoke to, what was said, and what was agreed — so a complaint that goes nowhere still leaves you with a paper trail.

How it works

Step by step

  1. Log every contact — date, person, channel (phone, email, in person) and what was actually said.
  2. Record what was agreed and by when, in the promises-and-follow-up tracker.
  3. Keep reference numbers, order numbers, or complaint IDs in one place.
  4. Use the completed log as the evidence base for a review, chargeback, or escalation.
Who it's for

Anyone escalating a restaurant complaint

If a complaint is taking more than one conversation to resolve, this keeps every promise made to you in one place you can point back to.

What's included
  • Chronological contact log (date, contact, channel, what was said)
  • Promises & follow-up tracker with deadlines
  • Key contacts field
  • Reference/complaint ID tracker — delivered as PDF + Excel
