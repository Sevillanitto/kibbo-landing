Free Calculator

Insurance deductible calculator: how much will I pay?

Enter your deductible and your claim to see what you'll pay out of pocket and what your insurer covers. Works for a standard flat deductible and as a hurricane deductible calculator — for the percentage deductibles that can turn a storm claim into a five-figure bill.

Percentage deductibles are common for hurricane, named-storm and wind damage on home insurance. Not sure which you have? Check your policy's declarations page.
This is your home's insured value as stated on your policy declarations page — not its market value or what you paid for it. These are often different numbers.

Please enter the claim amount and your deductible — and for a percentage deductible, a percentage between 0 and 100 plus your insured dwelling value.
