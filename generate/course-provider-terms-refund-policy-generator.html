For Course Providers
Write the policy once, post it everywhere · Free

Reduce your own risk of a complaint.

This tool is for course creators, bootcamp operators, and academy owners — not students. Answer a few questions about your program and the policy you need, and get a clear, learner-facing policy document ready for your website or enrollment page.

What to mention for each document (pick one per document)

  • Enrollment & Cancellation Policy: cancellation window with no penalty, penalty structure after that window, what happens if you cancel or postpone a cohort
  • Refund Policy: refund method and processing time, prorated vs. full refund conditions, any non-refundable components (e.g. a registration fee)
  • Certificate/Completion Policy: what specifically counts as "completion" (attendance %, passing assessment, project submission), whether your certificate is accredited by a named third-party body or is an internal completion certificate only, and how that distinction is communicated to buyers
  • Marketing Claims Disclaimer: any employment/outcome statistics you want to reference, and whether those statistics are independently verified or self-reported
