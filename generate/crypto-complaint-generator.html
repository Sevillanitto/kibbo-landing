Crypto Complaint Generator
Routed to the regulator that actually handles your problem · Free

There's no single "crypto regulator" — the right agency depends on the problem.

Answer a few questions and get a formal complaint letter routed correctly for your country and your specific problem type. In the US alone, a frozen exchange account, a suspected scam, and a debanked account each go to a different agency (FTC, SEC, FinCEN, CFPB, or the OCC) — this generator never collapses that into one guess. The UK routes to the FCA and FOS, the EU to your own country's national MiCA authority (not a single EU-wide body), and Australia to ASIC, AFCA, or Scamwatch depending on the issue.
