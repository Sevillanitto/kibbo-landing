What to mention for each policy (pick one per document)

  • Reservation Policy: online/phone/both booking, max group size without deposit, grace period before releasing a table
  • Cancellation Policy: minimum notice for free cancellation, penalty (% or fixed amount), exceptions (e.g. emergencies)
  • No-Show Policy: deposit required, deposit policy on no-show, number of no-shows before requiring a deposit on future bookings
  • Refund Policy: refund method (cash/card/store credit), processing time, qualifying cases (undeclared allergen, poor service, billing error)
  • Allergen Policy: shared-kitchen cross-contact risk, procedure when a customer discloses an allergy
  • Delivery Policy: own delivery or platform (Uber Eats/Deliveroo/etc.), estimated delivery time, policy for incorrect/cold orders
  • Privacy Policy: booking data collected (name/email/phone), whether email is used for marketing, data shared with third parties (booking/delivery platforms)
