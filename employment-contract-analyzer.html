What's really in your employment contract?

Upload your employment contract as a PDF. We'll flag non-compete clauses broader than the role justifies, worker misclassification red flags, missing severance terms, unusually long probation periods, training-repayment clawbacks, and overbroad IP/confidentiality clauses.

3 free analyses per day · No account required
