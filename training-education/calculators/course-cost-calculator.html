Course cost calculator: the real cost of a bootcamp or training program

Tuition is only part of the bill. If you can't work while studying full-time, the income you give up is often the single biggest — and least visible — cost. This also works as an income share agreement (ISA) calculator: estimate what an ISA would really cost you, based on your own expected salary.

Materials or equipment, exam or certification fees, relocation or housing if applicable — one combined total. Leave at 0 if none.
Used to work out the income you'd give up by not working during the program. Leave blank to skip this part of the calculation.
Typically 8%–25% of gross monthly income, most commonly 9%–17%.
Commonly $35,000–$50,000/year (or the equivalent in your currency).
Commonly 12–48 months.
Many ISAs cap total repayment at 1.5x–2x the sticker tuition. Be cautious of any ISA with no cap at all, or a cap above 2x. Leave blank if there's no cap or you don't know it.
Used to estimate your actual ISA cost below. Run this twice — once with a conservative (lower) salary and once with an optimistic (higher) one — to see the real range, not just one number.

Please fill in the currency, tuition, program length and schedule, and — for an ISA — the income share percentage, threshold and payment term.
