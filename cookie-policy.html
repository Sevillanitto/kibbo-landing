Cookie Policy

How Kibbo uses cookies and similar technologies, and how to control them.

What this page covers

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies (like browser local storage) Kibbo uses, why, and how you can control them. It applies to getkibbo.com and all its subpages.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, like browser "local storage," work the same way but aren't technically cookies — we cover both here since they serve the same purpose: remembering something about your visit.

What we use, specifically

Analytics — Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: which pages are popular, how people navigate, and where to improve. This sets cookies that collect anonymized usage data. Google's own privacy policy governs how they process this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Functional — Generator access

When you unlock a generator by providing your email, we store a flag in your browser's local storage so you don't have to provide your email again on future visits to other generators. This isn't a cookie in the strict technical sense, but it works similarly and is covered by this policy. No personal data beyond this unlock flag is stored locally — your email itself is stored securely in our email service provider (Mailchimp), not in your browser.

What we don't use

Kibbo doesn't run advertising, and we don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. We don't sell or share cookie data with advertisers.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Get notified when a cookie is set

Blocking Google Analytics cookies won't affect your ability to use Kibbo's tools, templates, or content — it only limits the usage data we can see. Clearing your browser's local storage will mean generators ask for your email again on your next visit, since the unlock flag will be gone.

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools and services change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@getkibbo.com.
