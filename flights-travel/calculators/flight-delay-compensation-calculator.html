Free Calculator

Flight delay compensation calculator — UK, US & Canada

Free, instant calculator covering the jurisdictions EU261 doesn't: UK261, US DOT rules, and Canada's Air Passenger Protection Regulations (APPR). Answer a few quick questions and see exactly what you're owed — or, in the US, exactly what right you actually have.

Flying to/from the EU instead? Use the EU261 Compensation Calculator.
Not sure? Check the distance on Google Maps or greatcirclemapper.net.

There's no per-hour cash compensation like EU261/UK261 in the US — DOT rules give you a refund right and procedural protections instead, not a payout. This calculator will never show you a dollar compensation figure for a US flight.

APPR applies when Canada is your departure point, or you're arriving on a Canadian carrier. UK261 and APPR can't both apply to the same flight — only one jurisdiction, based on where you departed, governs.
