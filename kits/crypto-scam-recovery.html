Crypto Scam Recovery Kit

Three tools, in the order you'd actually need them — every one of them completely free to use.

This kit follows the real sequence after a crypto scam: confirm the scam pattern and document it — wallet addresses, transaction IDs, messages, websites — then file the formal complaint, then secure any remaining assets to prevent further loss. Every tool in this kit is completely free to use — no account, no payment, no catch.

The sequence

Be wary of anyone who contacts you offering to recover your crypto for a fee — "recovery services" are one of the most common follow-up scams targeting victims.
