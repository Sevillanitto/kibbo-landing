Subscription & Gym Cancellation Kit

Six tools, in the order you'd actually need them — not a list to figure out yourself. Every tool in this kit is completely free to use.

This kit follows the real sequence of the problem: cancel the subscription or membership properly, dispute it in writing if a charge lands anyway, and escalate formally if that dispute gets ignored. Each step links straight to the exact tool built for it — plus the checklists and template most people need alongside the letters. Every generator, checklist and template below is completely free — no account, no payment, no catch.

The sequence

Start at step 1 regardless of where the problem currently stands — each letter is written to reference the one before it.

Step 1 · Cancel

End the subscription or membership

A firm cancellation letter with a clear cancellation date on record — for an ongoing subscription, a free trial, or a fixed-term gym or service contract.

  1. 1Subscription & Service Cancellation Generator →

Step 2 · Still charged?

Dispute the charge

If a charge lands after that cancellation date — a post-cancellation charge, a price increase without proper notice, a duplicate charge, or an unwanted renewal — dispute it formally in writing.

Step 3 · No response?

Escalate it

If the dispute above goes nowhere, escalate with a formal complaint that references what you already sent and demands a specific, timely response.
