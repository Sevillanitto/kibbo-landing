Wrongful Charge After Cancellation Kit

Already cancelled, but charged anyway? Dispute it, escalate it, and get it refunded — every tool in this kit is completely free to use.

This kit is for a different moment than the Subscription & Gym Cancellation Kit: it's not for cancelling a subscription or membership — it's for when you already cancelled and got charged anyway. Start with the billing dispute, escalate formally if it's ignored or refused, and use the checklists and evidence pack below to build your case and claim a refund. Every generator, checklist and template on this page is completely free to use — no account, no payment, no catch.

Haven't actually cancelled yet? Start with the Subscription & Gym Cancellation Kit instead — cancel first, then come back here only if a charge lands anyway.
