ACTION KIT · FINANCE & BANKING

Unauthorized Transaction / Card Fraud Kit

Seven tools, in the order you'd actually need them — every one of them completely free to use.

This kit follows the real sequence of a fraud or disputed-charge problem: report the unauthorized transaction to your bank, dispute the specific charge if it isn't clearly fraud, and escalate formally if the bank refuses or drags its feet. Every generator, template and checklist below is completely free to use — no account, no payment, no catch.

The sequence

Start at step 1 for a transaction you never made — jump to step 2 if it's a specific charge you did authorize but dispute the amount or delivery of.

Step 1 · Report it

Dispute the unauthorized transaction

A firm dispute letter for a transaction you never made — a lost/stolen card or a compromised account — requesting an investigation and reversal. The main tool in this kit.

Step 2 · Not clearly fraud?

Dispute the specific charge

For a merchant billing problem instead — never received, not as described, a duplicate charge, or charged after you cancelled — rather than an outright unauthorized transaction.

Step 3 · Refused or ignored?

Escalate to the bank

A firm, formal complaint to your bank or financial institution if the dispute above is refused, dragged out, or handled poorly by staff.
