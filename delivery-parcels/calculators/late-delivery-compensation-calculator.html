Late delivery compensation calculator: can I get a refund for a late package?

Here's the part most people don't expect: most shipping services carry no guarantee of on-time delivery at all. No guarantee means no compensation for lateness itself — only for a package that's lost or damaged. Only specific guaranteed or express service tiers offer a money-back guarantee, and even then it typically refunds just the shipping cost, never what was inside the box.

Please choose your carrier and service, say whether it was late, and enter what you paid for shipping.
