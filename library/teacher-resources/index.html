Datasets for the classroom

4 available

Kibbo Streaming Pricing Dataset, 2011–2026

97 verified price-change events — launches, increases, the one decrease, and tier restructures — across 10 major US streaming platforms, from each platform's launch through September 2026, with the before and after price and percentage change for every event.

Open in the Consumer Data Library →

US Airline Reliability Dataset — 2026 H1

On-time performance, cancellation rates, mishandled baggage, and involuntary denied boarding data for the 9 major US airlines, January–June 2026. Split into three files: the main ranking, a year-over-year comparison, and Spirit Airlines' monthly on-time rate before it ceased operations.

Open in the Consumer Data Library →

Kibbo Healthcare Cost Dataset, 2026

58 data points comparing the cost of getting sick in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany and Spain — what you pay in advance, what four common treatments cost, and waiting times — across three cost layers, with every gap that couldn't be verified marked rather than estimated.

Open in the Consumer Data Library →

The CFPB Complaint Vault Dataset

All 18.2 million complaints with harmonised product families, template classification for every narrative, company business types, complaints per 100,000 residents by state, and bank complaint rates — plus a data dictionary, the full code and SHA-256 checksums.

Open in the Consumer Data Library →
